# Aura 0.9.0 Preview Release

## Goal

Release Aura 0.9.0 as a technical preview after phases 2, 3, and 4 of the
plan to bring Aura to Rust speed on the 30 cross-language programs. On
2026-10-29 John accepted `task_pool` at 2.4x Rust (its 1.5x gate is met
only with one worker) and asked to release without re-timing on the Mac.

## Cutoff

The candidate is cut from `main` after step 4d (PR #185) merged. It holds
the rest of Phase 2 (through #175), Phase 3 (#176 to #181), and Phase 4
steps 4a to 4d (#182 to #185).

## Measurements

On the shared Linux x86-64 container with 4 vCPUs, median of five runs at
the bench size, at the 4d head (the candidate without its docs). The
table for each program is in `benchmarks/cross_language/README.md`.

| Measure | Aura 0.8.0 | End of phase 2 | End of phase 3 | Aura 0.9.0 |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| All 30 programs, wall time | 147 s | 48.5 s | 27.9 s | 23.5 s |
| Median Aura / Rust | 16x | 8.2x | 4.8x | 4.6x |
| Median Aura / Python 3.13 | 2.7x | 0.71x | 0.51x | 0.53x |
| Programs faster than Python | 9 | 19 | 20 | 22 |

Closest to Rust: `http_api` 1.0x, `kv_server` 1.2x, `bytecode_vm`,
`hashing`, `image_filters`, and `pubsub_broker` 1.4x. Furthest:
`sql_engine` 22x, `binary_trees` and `regex_match` 10x. The instruction
counts and operation probes were not re-recorded for this release; the
README tables for them are from 0.8.0.

## Release

- PR #186 merged as `b53a7ee9`. The annotated `v0.9.0-preview` tag object
  `16282942` points at it. It is unsigned by choice because no repository
  signing identity is configured. It was pushed on 2026-10-29 from John's
  checkout after he approved the tag push.
- Release run `40952894283` passed all seven jobs. It published the Linux
  x86-64, macOS arm64, and macOS x86-64 archives, the docs, the VSIX, and
  `SHA256SUMS`, and published extension 0.9.0 to the Visual Studio
  Marketplace and Open VSX.
- The Linux archive matches `SHA256SUMS`, its `aura --version` prints
  `aura 0.9.0-preview (b53a7ee9b80e)`, and it builds and runs hello world
  (974,168 bytes).
